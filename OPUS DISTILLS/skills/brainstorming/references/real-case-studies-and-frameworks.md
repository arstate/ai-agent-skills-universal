## 1. Real-World Marketing & Brand Strategy Case Studies (Global + Indonesia)

### Case Study 1: WePay's Block of Ice — The 3-Rule Guerrilla Masterclass
- **The Context:** Early-stage fintech startup WePay needed to steal attention at a PayPal developer conference where PayPal had a massive sponsor budget.
- **The Brainstormed Play:** Instead of buying a boring booth, WePay froze actual cash inside a giant 600-pound block of ice and wheeled it right in front of the conference venue with a bold sign: **"PayPal freezes your accounts. Unfreeze your money with WePay."**
- **The Result:** 300% surge in daily signups, viral press coverage across TechCrunch and mainstream media, near-zero customer acquisition cost.
- **Why It Worked (3 Guerrilla Rules from `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`):**
  1. **Focused:** Targeted the exact high-intent audience (frustrated PayPal merchants/developers) at a single location.
  2. **On-Brand:** Directly dramatized the #1 pain point of the competitor ("frozen funds") vs WePay's core value prop (instant liquidity).
  3. **Talkable:** Impossible to walk past without taking a photo and posting it online.
- **How to Adapt for Indonesian Brands:** Identify the #1 unspoken frustration in your industry (e.g., hidden KPR admin fees in property, slow 14-day agency revisions in creative services) and dramatize that contrast visually in a 3-second hook or physical/digital stunt.

### Case Study 2: Stripe Press & Linear — Design Quality as a Category Moat
- **The Context:** B2B developer tools and financial infrastructure were historically ugly, dense, and enterprise-gray.
- **The Brainstormed Play:**
  - **Linear** rejected generic bright SaaS templates and built an ultra-fast, dark-mode obsidian cockpit (`#08090A`) with `1px` hairline borders, keyboard-first command palettes (`⌘K`), and `150ms` cubic-bezier micro-transitions—positioning project management as a high-precision craft tool.
  - **Stripe Press** created a tactile digital bookstore featuring rotatable 3D WebGL book spines, Swiss grid typography, and vibrant book-cover color palettes—turning dry engineering leadership content into collectible status objects.
- **The Result:** Massive organic word-of-mouth among designers and CTOs; design quality itself became the primary trust signal for technical reliability.
- **How to Adapt for Web/UI Brainstorming:** Never make a B2B or high-ticket service website look like a generic template. Use **Archetype 2 (Swiss Hairline Grid)** or **Archetype 6 (Command Cockpit)** so the interface itself proves engineering and creative superiority within 500ms.

### Case Study 3: Liquid Death & Duolingo — Contrarian Category Positioning & "The Pratfall Effect"
- **The Context:** Canned water is the most commoditized, boring product on earth; language-learning apps suffer from high user drop-off and polite, forgettable notifications.
- **The Brainstormed Play:**
  - **Liquid Death** packaged plain mountain water in tallboy heavy-metal beer cans with the slogan *"Murder Your Thirst"*, allowing non-drinkers at concerts and bars to hold something that looked as rebellious as an energy drink or beer.
  - **Duolingo** leaned into the unhinged, passive-aggressive green owl persona on TikTok—using self-deprecating humor and absurd comment replies rather than polished corporate ads.
- **The Result:** Liquid Death reached a $1.4B valuation selling water; Duolingo generated billions of organic TikTok impressions with near-zero paid media spend.
- **Psychological Mechanism:** **Category Contrast + The Pratfall Effect** (brands that show bold personality or playful self-awareness become 2x–3x more likable and memorable than sterile "perfect" corporate brands).

### Case Study 4: Apex Property (Surabaya & Sidoarjo) — "Cicilan Setara Ngopi" & KPR Transparency Play
- **The Context:** First-time homebuyers in Surabaya/Sidoarjo (Gen-Z & young millennial families) are terrified of KPR rejection (SLIK OJK) and overwhelmed by brochure specs ("LT 72 / LB 45, Bata Merah").
- **The Brainstormed Play:**
  1. **Reframing the Price Anchor (Coffee Cup Framing):** Instead of shouting *"Rumah Rp 400 Jutaan"*, reframe the daily outlay: *"Rp 90 ribuan sehari—seharga 2 kopi susu + parkir, tapi 15 tahun lagi jadi sertifikat SHM atas nama lu sendiri, bukan memperkaya ibu kos."*
  2. **Interactive KPR Joint-Income Pre-Check as Hero:** Instead of a static property brochure site, put a **30-Second Kalkulator Gaji Gabungan (Suami + Istri) & Cek Aman SLIK** right in the hero section, routing directly to Mbak Ningsih on WhatsApp with pre-filled simulation data.
  3. **100% UTJ Refund Guarantee (Risk Reversal):** Eliminate survey/booking fear by highlighting *"Garansi 100% UTJ Kembali Utuh kalau KPR ditolak bank BUMN."*
- **Psychological Mechanism:** **Pennies-a-Day Framing + Activation Energy Reduction + Zero-Risk Reversal**.

### Case Study 5: Avant Studio — The "3-Minute Live Ad & Web Leakage Audit" B2B Hook
- **The Context:** Indonesian SME/brand owners ignore cold WhatsApp messages offering *"Jasa Kelola Sosmed & Pembuatan Website Murah"*.
- **The Brainstormed Play:**
  1. **Give Value Before Asking (Reciprocity + Loss Aversion):** Before pitching a retainer, send a forensic 3-bubble WhatsApp breakdown showing a **specific revenue leak** on the prospect's current Instagram/Meta Ads or website (e.g., *"Kak, web kakak waktu dibuka dari IG Ads loading-nya 6.2 detik dan belum ada event Pixel AddToCart—artinya sekitar 40% klik iklan berbayar kakak bocor sebelum sempat chat CS"*).
  2. **The Barbell Service Model:** Pair a low-friction **Creative Testing Sprint (Rp 50rb/hari ad test)** with a high-ticket **Full-Stack Growth & Web Retainer**.
- **Psychological Mechanism:** **Specific Loss Aversion** (people act 2.5x faster to stop an active money leak than to chase a vague growth promise) + **Reciprocity**.

## 2. The 12 High-Leverage Behavioral Psychology Models (`marketing-psychology`)

When brainstorming any design, layout, copy, or strategy, weave at least 2 of these models into your recommendations:

| # | Mental Model | How It Works | Concrete Application in Design / Marketing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Activation Energy** | Every extra click, field, or cognitive step drops conversion exponentially. | Replace 8-field contact forms with a 2-click interactive selector that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. |
| 2 | **Loss Aversion** | Losing Rp 1.000.000 hurts 2.2x more than gaining Rp 1.000.000 feels good. | Frame audits around *"Berapa banyak leads/omzet yang bocor tiap bulan"* rather than generic *"Tingkatkan penjualan"*. |
| 3 | **Anchoring & Contrast** | The first number seen sets the mental benchmark for all following numbers. | Anchor against the cost of hiring an in-house team (Rp 25 Jt/bln) before revealing your agency retainer (Rp 6 Jt/bln). |
| 4 | **The IKEA Effect / Effort Investment** | Users value outcomes 3x more when they customized or interacted with them first. | Let users toggle sliders on a live ROI/KPR calculator before showing the final recommendation. |
| 5 | **Hick's Law & Choice Paradox** | More than 3–4 choices causes decision paralysis. | Cap pricing tables at 3 tiers; cap viewport CTA buttons to 1 Primary + 1 Ghost action. |
| 6 | **The Pratfall Effect** | Admitting a small, honest limitation makes every other claim 100% more believable. | *"Kami bukan agensi termurah dan hanya terima maksimal 4 klien baru per bulan supaya riset iklan nggak asal-asalan."* |
| 7 | **Zeigarnik Effect (Open Loop)** | Unfinished tasks or unanswered visual questions stay stuck in working memory. | Start carousels/videos with a visual anomaly or unfinished teardown (*"3 dari 4 iklan properti di Sidoarjo boncos karena 1 kesalahan di detik ke-2..."*). |
| 8 | **Specificity Bias** | Exact numbers (`38.4%`, `14 hari`, `Rp 3.850/lead`) beat round numbers (`40%`, `cepat`, `murah`) every time. | Ban round marketing claims in headlines; always use forensic numbers, timestamps, and named locations. |
| 9 | **Risk Reversal** | Shifting risk from buyer to seller unlocks stalled high-ticket decisions. | 100% UTJ refund if KPR fails; free 7-day creative hook replacement if CTR < 1.2%. |
| 10 | ** peak-End Rule** | People judge an entire experience by its most intense peak moment and its final ending. | Make the post-submit confirmation state or onboarding welcome screen unforgettable, not a plain `"Terima kasih"` text. |
| 11 | **Barbell Strategy (80/20)** | Put 80% of resources into proven, predictable channels and 20% into high-upside asymmetric experiments. | Always structure marketing brainstorms into **Core Engine (80%)** + **Outlier Stunt/Experiment (20%)**. |
| 12 | **Job-To-Be-Done (JTBD) Switching Trigger** | Customers don't buy products; they "hire" them during a specific life/business trigger moment. | Map campaigns to the exact trigger event (e.g., baby on the way -> need 2BR house; Meta Ads CPR spiking above Rp 15.000 -> need creative refresh). |

### Case Study 6: Tadaima Japanese Bakery & Insightory "Falco" — Turning Lucky Viral Hits into a Repeatable FYP System (`7.6M`, `5.1M`, `4.2M` Views)
- **The Problem:** Most brands treat TikTok/Reels like gambling—celebrating vanity view counts without knowing *why* a post worked, leading to inconsistent flops on the next post.
- **The 5-Step Viral Engineering Playbook:**
  1. **Compare Winners AND Losers (`100–200 Posts`):** Export both top-performing and flopped posts so the contrast reveals what actually drives results.
  2. **Multimodal Scene Transcription:** Combine metrics with frame-by-frame visual/hook descriptions (`0–3s` physical action, `3–8s` mystery trigger, `8–14s` reaction in store setting, `14–18s` promo reveal).
  3. **Rank by True-Winning Metrics (Save Rate, Share Rate, Play-Through Retention):** Ignore empty views; rank by **Saves (`237k saves`)**, **Shares (`123k shares`)**, and **Play-Through Rate (`> 50%`)**.
  4. **Codify into a Beat-by-Beat Recipe Template:** Turn the winning pattern (*Mystery -> Slapstick Reaction -> Promo as Twist Punchline*, zero ad-scent in the first 10 seconds, 100% mute-friendly physical hook, 1 single offer `"Beli 4 Gratis 1"`) into a repeatable `0–18s` beat table.
  5. **Validate via 3 Controlled Variations:** Test 3 variations of the exact same structural recipe across different scenarios to prove the system works reliably on command.
