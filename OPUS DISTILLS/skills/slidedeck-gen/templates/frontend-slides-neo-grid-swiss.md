## Principles of Structured Modernism
Eliminating decorative visual clutter in favor of typographic clarity, structural honesty, and functional density.

- **Typographic Discipline**: Strict hierarchy using grotesque grotesque type scales and intentional contrast.
- **Bento Card Matrix**: Modular self-contained information blocks that scale seamlessly across form factors.
- **Content-First Composition**: Every element earns its place through utility; zero decorative filler.

## The Architecture of Clarity
Form follows function, but clarity precedes form. When information is structured with mathematical rigor, comprehension follows naturally.

- **Asymmetric Grid Balance**: Guiding user attention through conscious spatial weighting rather than superficial accents.
- **Systematic Spacing Tokens**: 8pt grid consistency binding all layout elements into a coherent whole.
- **High-Contrast Telemetry**: Sharp boundaries and crisp delimiters that respect executive cognitive bandwidth.

> "Good design is as little design as possible. Less, but better — because it concentrates on the essential aspects."
